Tableau story: on-time performance by carrier, short-haul shipments (0-300 miles).

Jan-Feb 2026

Carrier cost and performance optimization

Business problem
How can shipping cost be reduced without hurting delivery reliability?
What I did
Analyzed 10K+ shipment records by carrier and distance, comparing cost and on-time performance with Python, Pandas and SQL, and visualized it in Tableau.
Key finding
On short-haul shipments (0-300 miles), UPS had the lowest on-time rate at 93.75%, followed by FedEx at 94.12%. Amazon Logistics, DHL, LaserShip and USPS were on time 100% of the time.
Recommendation
Use distance-based carrier allocation, with a projected 15-20% shipment-cost reduction.

Tableau story with Claude AI insights. Data modeled in Snowflake Bronze, Silver and Gold layers.

Apr-May 2026

E-commerce analytics with AI insights

Business problem
How can raw e-commerce orders become reliable revenue, customer, product and category KPIs?
What I did
Built Bronze, Silver and Gold layers in Snowflake for 550K+ orders and used SQL with CTEs to generate the KPIs.
Key insights
Seasonal demand drops after peak months, which points to a need for a retention strategy. São Paulo dominates order volume but underperforms on customer spend value.
Outcome
Visualized the KPIs in Tableau and used Claude AI to explore and communicate patterns in the results.
