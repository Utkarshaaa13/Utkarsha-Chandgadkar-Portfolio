Power BI dashboard: carrier cost and on-time delivery performance overview.

Jan-Feb 2026

Carrier Cost and Delivery Performance Analysis

Business problem
Which carriers provide the best and weakest value when shipping cost and delivery performance are evaluated together, and how does performance vary across distance segments?
What I did
Analyzed 2,000 shipment records across 7 carriers and built an interactive Power BI dashboard using Power Query and DAX to evaluate shipping cost, on-time delivery, shipment outcomes and performance across distance segments.
Key insights
The 301-600 mile segment had the weakest overall on-time performance at 84.98%. Carrier performance also varied meaningfully by distance, showing that cost and reliability should be evaluated by segment rather than through one overall ranking.
Outcome
Developed distance-based carrier allocation recommendations, including a controlled test of eligible 301-600 mile volume toward stronger-performing options before scaling changes.

Apr-May 2026

E-commerce analytics with AI insights

Business problem
How can raw e-commerce orders become reliable revenue, customer, product and category KPIs?
What I did
Built Bronze, Silver and Gold layers in Snowflake for 550K+ orders and used SQL with CTEs to generate the KPIs.
Key insights
Seasonal demand drops after peak months, which points to a need for a retention strategy. São Paulo dominates order volume but underperforms on customer spend value.
Outcome
Visualized the KPIs in Tableau and used Claude AI to explore and communicate patterns in the results.

Nov-Dec 2024

AWS data pipeline and analytics

Business problem
How can raw files be prepared for reliable analysis?
What I did
Ingested data into S3, cleaned it with DataBrew, cataloged it with a Glue crawler and Data Catalog, transformed it with a Glue ETL job in PySpark, and queried it with Athena.
Key insights
A layered AWS workflow separates raw ingestion, data-quality preparation, cataloging and transformation, while Parquet provides a structured analytics-ready layer for downstream querying.
Outcome
Built an end-to-end AWS analytics workflow from raw S3 ingestion through transformation and cataloging to Athena and QuickSight consumption.
